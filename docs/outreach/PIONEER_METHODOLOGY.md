# Sangoma Pioneer 100 Identification Methodology

This document formalizes the methodology used to identify, categorize, and prioritize the 'Pioneer 100' cohort for the Sangoma Protocol pilot phase.

## 1. Objective
The goal of the Pioneer 100 program is to onboard a high-trust, diverse group of South African domain experts and influencers to:
*   Stress-test the protocol's matching engine and settlement logic.
*   Provide a 'truth layer' for initial market resolution.
*   Build early liquidity and community trust in a regulated sandbox environment.

## 2. Selection Criteria
Candidates were selected based on three primary pillars:

### A. Domain Expertise (Skin in the Game)
*   **Economics & Finance:** Chief economists, wealth managers, and fintech innovators who understand market mechanics and hedging.
*   **Politics & Policy:** Analysts and researchers with a deep understanding of South African legislative and electoral cycles.
*   **Energy & Infrastructure:** Experts tracking Eskom, loadshedding, and infrastructure stability.

### B. Network & Influence
*   **Media & Journalism:** Influential editors and business journalists capable of amplifying the protocol's mission.
*   **Crypto/DeFi Native:** Leaders in the local CASP (Crypto Asset Service Provider) ecosystem and blockchain educators.
*   **Community Leaders:** Figures with high social trust who can bridge the gap between complex DeFi tools and everyday utility.

### C. Regulatory & Compliance Alignment
*   Targets include current and former members of regulatory bodies (SARB, FSCA) to ensure the protocol remains aligned with IFWG sandbox requirements.

## 3. Categorization & Distribution
The Pioneer 100 cohort is divided into the following key sectors:
1.  **Finance/Economy:** ~30%
2.  **Media/Broadcasting:** ~25%
3.  **Crypto/Tech:** ~20%
4.  **Politics/Legal/Regulatory:** ~15%
5.  **Industry/Energy/Civil Society:** ~10%

## 4. Prioritization Logic (Waves)
The directory is prioritized into waves to manage outreach bandwidth and system stability:
*   **Priority 1 (Wave 1):** High-reach influencers and critical domain experts (e.g., VALR CEO, Chief Economists, top journalists).
*   **Priority 2 (Wave 2):** Institutional representatives and legal experts.
*   **Priority 3 (Wave 3):** Broad community figures and specialized academic researchers.

## 5. Maintenance
The directory is maintained in `/home/team/shared/pioneer_directory_final.csv`. Any additions or modifications must follow the categorization logic defined above to maintain a balanced cohort.
